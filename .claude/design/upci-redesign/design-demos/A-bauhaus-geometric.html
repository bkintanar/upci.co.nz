About the UPCI NZ

A national family of Apostolic Pentecostal churches

UPCI New Zealand is the national body of the United Pentecostal Church International in New Zealand — ten affiliated churches, organised into three regions, served by six national departments and an Apostolic Bible College.

Our structure is simple by design: local churches remain the centre of ministry, the regions connect neighbouring congregations, and the national body exists to serve both.
