Who we are

The Apostolic Men's Department of UPCI New Zealand is a ministry dedicated to building strong, Spirit-led men who are grounded in Apostolic doctrine and committed to living a life that honours God.

Our mission

Our mission is to equip men to lead with integrity in their homes, churches, and communities by fostering spiritual growth, discipleship, and servant leadership. Through regular gatherings, mentorship, and ministry opportunities, we encourage men to deepen their relationship with God, develop godly character, and stand firm in faith.

Our focus

We believe that men play a vital role in strengthening families and advancing the Kingdom of God. As such, the department provides a supportive brotherhood where men can be challenged, encouraged, and empowered to fulfil their calling. Whether through prayer, teaching, outreach, or fellowship, the Apostolic Men's Department exists to inspire men to be faithful, steadfast, and ready always to serve.
