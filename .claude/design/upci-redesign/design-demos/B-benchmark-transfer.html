About the UPCI NZ

Who we are

UPCI New Zealand is the national body of the United Pentecostal Church International in New Zealand — ten affiliated churches, organised into three regions, supported by six national departments and the Apostolic Bible College. Our work is coordinated nationally so that each local church can be resourced, connected and represented as part of one body.

The national executive is responsible for oversight across all three regions, for the departments that serve members nationwide, and for the calendar of conferences, trainings and gatherings that bring the churches together each year.
